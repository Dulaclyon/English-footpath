# Curriculum research and expansion map

Research date: 2026-10-27. The app's sequence is a proposed teaching scaffold, not a scraped textbook or a claim that every Chinese school follows the same annual sequence.

## Primary sources

1. Ministry of Education release of the compulsory education standards (2022): https://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A26/s8001/202204/t20220420_619921.html
2. Official Institute of Curriculum and Textbook Research download library: https://www.ictr.edu.cn/download_center/ywjy/p/1.html
3. Senior High English Curriculum Standard (2017, revised 2020), published by People's Education Press: https://www.pep.com.cn/xw/zt/rjwy/gzkb2020/202205/P020220517522153664167.pdf

The first release's web retrieval found the compulsory standard in the official index, but its full PDF exceeded the web tool's retrieval limit. The senior-high PDF was accessible. The broad compulsory attainment bands are Grades 3–4 (Level 1), 5–6 (Level 2), 7–9 (Level 3). High-school course requirements are organised by compulsory and selective/elective courses, not a universal grammar list for each school year. Avoid treating Gaokao preparation as a substitute for listening, speaking, reading, viewing and writing.

## Proposed full pathway

| Year | Language and grammar strands for expansion | Texts and application |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 3 | Sound-letter awareness, phonics, classroom instructions, greetings, pronouns, be, basic nouns, colours and numbers | Labelled pictures, dialogues, very short stories; identify people and objects |
| Grade 4 | Simple present, have/has, can, singular/plural, a/an, possessives, where and basic prepositions | Home, school, food, routines; follow simple descriptions |
| Grade 5 | Present continuous, there is/are, countability, some/any, time and frequency | Descriptions, invitations, daily events; notice actions and sequence |
| Grade 6 | Past simple, future plans, question formation, basic conjunctions, comparative review | Recounts, short letters, travel plans; retell and give simple reasons |
| Grade 7 | Consolidate tense and sentence patterns, comparatives/superlatives, modals, quantity | Personal and community texts; connect ideas and compare choices |
| Grade 8 | Past continuous, present perfect, adverbial clauses, infinitive/gerund introduction | Narratives, experiences, advice; distinguish timelines and causes |
| Grade 9 | Passive, reported speech, introductory relative/object clauses, tense consolidation | Explanations, social topics; infer meaning, summarise and justify |
| Senior High 1 | Relative clauses, expanded tense/aspect, non-finite forms, lexical chunks and cohesion | Longer narratives and expository texts; analyse structure and paraphrase |
| Senior High 2 | Noun clauses, complex clause relationships, non-finite structures, stance and evidence | Argumentation and authentic adapted texts; evaluate evidence and perspectives |
| Senior High 3 | Hypotheticals, nuanced modality, ellipsis/inversion where useful, integrated review | Extended reading, synthesis and continuation writing; handle ambiguity and transfer |

The deployed starter samples one grammar milestone per row, three original texts per milestone, three application questions per text, and a repeated retrieval bank. The table above describes further authoring scope, not content already present in the app. Real classroom alignment should specify textbook edition, region, learner baseline and teacher review.

## Repetition design

The requested product rule is 50 retrieval submissions per milestone, optionally 60. Count each submitted response once; count errors separately from correct responses; never treat simply opening a text as retrieval. Learners must complete reading questions and pass a 10-item check at 80% before the next milestone opens. Errors are prioritised in practice, and reviews are scheduled locally.

This numerical rule does not guarantee familiarity or durable mastery. The initial bank intentionally repeats and is too small for a secure assessment. Expansion should provide many contexts per knowledge point, sentence construction, expression retrieval, delayed retention checks and speaking/writing tasks. Vocabulary and useful expressions currently appear as supports; they do not yet have independent 50-attempt mastery gates. Children should practise in short sessions with breaks rather than 50 uninterrupted repetitions.

## Release limits

No online learner accounts or cross-device sync. No recording of children's voices. Device-generated speech is optional. Browser/device installation should be validated on physical iPhone and Android hardware before a classroom rollout. Native store releases are not included.
