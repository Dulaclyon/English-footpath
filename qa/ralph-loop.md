## Cycle 1 — learning and account boundaries
Primary objective: verify that scored attempts, balanced content categories, reading prerequisites and mastery checks control advancement; verify account ownership boundaries locally.
Pre-cycle corrections: balanced category minima (15 grammar, 15 vocabulary, 10 expressions, 10 reading); live practice counters; deduplicated submissions; backup preservation for workshop/IELTS progress; account requests excluded from offline caches.
Evidence: Node learning tests passed for all 10 lesson views, content totals, 50/60 targets, category balance, reading prerequisites, duplicate submissions and failed/passed mastery. Local HTTP account tests passed: anonymous and student access denied to teacher endpoints; teacher ownership enforced; password reset invalidates sessions; progress isolated between accounts; stale revisions rejected.
